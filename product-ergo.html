Ergo

Farmer comfort and safety are never negotiable, since food security depends on it. GatorCorp's agricultural seats are engineered with the working farmer in mind, combining field- and lab-tested design to deliver class-leading vibration absorption and all-day comfort. The Ergo range spans a reclining air-suspension seat, an under-seat suspension (USS) seat, and a behind-back suspension (BBS) seat — each built to fit a different tractor and budget.
