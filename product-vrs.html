Atlas - II (VRS)

The Atlas - II (VRS) seat pairs ActiveVRS — an active magnetic damper with an integrated position sensor — reading the road 500 times a second and adjusting ride stiffness in real time. Where passive air suspension only reacts to bumps already underway, ActiveVRS anticipates them — cutting whole-body vibration well beyond what a standard suspension seat can manage, for drivers who spend eight, ten, twelve hours a day in the cab.
