Ultimo

The GatorCorp Ultimo is a premium-engineered truck seat built for long-haul and heavy-load driving, where continuous road vibration takes a toll on drivers. Its pneumatic suspension keeps vibration transmission low across long shifts, and the seat height adjusts continuously to suit each driver. The Luxury trim adds a wider seat top with built-in lumbar and thigh support, engineered for durability and tested against industry strength and reliability standards, with domestic and international homologation available on request.
