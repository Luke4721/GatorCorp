Duro

Durability, ergonomics, and comfort drove the design of the GatorCorp Duro backhoe loader seat. Built to shield operators from harsh vibration and long shifts in tough field conditions, Duro uses GatorCorp-Sears mechanical and air suspension technology suited to backhoe loaders and other construction and compaction machinery — time-proven designs that isolate vibration and reduce fatigue. The seat profile keeps both seat and machine controls within easy reach, with features like an operator presence switch, position sensors, and a seat belt supporting operator safety.
