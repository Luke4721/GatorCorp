Boss

The GatorCorp Boss is a rugged excavator seat built to absorb the jolts and low-frequency vibration that come with digging and grading all day. Its high, side-bolstered backrest combines with stepper height adjustment, backrest recline, and an adjustable headrest to keep operators supported through long shifts. Sears-based mechanical and air suspension options are engineered specifically for excavator-class vibration, and the seat is available with or without pod-control mounts for machines that route joystick controls through the seat itself. The Boss series fits everything from mini and compact excavators up to large, mid-sized machines.
